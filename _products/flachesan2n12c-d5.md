<p><strong>Key Features</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>All NVMe Flash Array supports Dual Intel® Xeon® SP Scalable Processors (Skylake)</li>
<li>Support 12 x front accessible hot-swap NVMe PCIe3 x8 Add-in-Cards</li>
<li>Up to 60GB/s (480 Gigabits/s) throughput and 12 Millions IOPS through RDMA</li>
<li>Support 4x full height PCIe3 x16 and 2x Low Profile PCIe3 x16 slots</li>
<li>Symmetrically balanced design, each CPU managing half NVMe and NIC</li>
</ul>
