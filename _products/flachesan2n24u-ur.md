<p><strong>Key Features</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>All NVMe Flash Array supports Single Socket 2nd Gen AMD EPYC up to 64 cores Single/Multi NUMA domain</li>
<li>Supports 24x front accessible hot-swap NVMe PCIe3 x4 2.5” drives</li>
<li>Up to 60GB/s (480 Gigabits/s) throughput and 12 Millions IOPS through RDMA</li>
<li>Supports 4x full height PCIe3 x16 PCIe slots (Optional: 6x PCIe3 x16 slots)</li>
<li>Symmetrically balance design each CPU managing half NVMe and NIC</li>
</ul>
