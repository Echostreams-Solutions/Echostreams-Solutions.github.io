When the Solution Requires Absolute Mission Critical Reliability

Mission critical servers are an essential factor to sustaining normal business operations. Any potential disruption or loss of functions will result in catastrophic consequences that may damage productivity, reputation, and financial cost of business. Enterprise operations relying on mission critical servers for the success of their business require dedicated hardware infrastructure with strict parameters to guarantee uptime as a physical system failure might become a complete disaster.

Every critical application depends on a certain degree of fault tolerance and for IT environments, this means a complete backup system is necessary to enable continuous operation in the event of primary hardware failure. This active redundancy duplication of critical components ensures high availability and reliability for mission critical objectives. Many server systems feature 1+1 redundant power supplies as a fail-safe solution for a consistent source of power. However mission critical servers necessitate redundancy of the entire system while allowing for hot swapping maintenance repair without interrupting system operation.

Dual-Node, Cluster-in-a-Box Design for High Availability

Our DuraStreams platforms employ dual-node system with a shared storage pool, also known as Cluster-in-a-Box platform. The system will have 2 hardware nodes each with its own motherboard, CPU, RAM, OS drives that can perform either active-active or active-passive redundancy depending on administrators requirements. These 2 hardware nodes share the same storage pool that has dual-ported drives such as 12G SAS drives or dual-ported NVMe drives, therefore each node will be able to read and write to the shared-storage pool.
